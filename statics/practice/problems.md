# Statics practice problems

[Learning path](../README.md) · [Worked answers](answers.md)

Attempt these on paper before opening the answers. For every body problem, draw the FBD, write signed equations, solve with units, and check equilibrium. Angles are in degrees; use $g=9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$. Right/up/counterclockwise are positive. All beams are horizontal, weightless, and rigid unless otherwise stated; beam force locations have $y=0$ unless specified. Supports and cables are ideal.

## Units and geometry

**P1. Weight.** A 12 kg object rests near Earth's surface. Find the magnitude and direction of its weight.

**P2. Moment units.** A 400 N downward force acts 250 mm right of O on a horizontal arm. Find the signed moment about O in N·m.

## Forces and vectors

**P3. Angle from vertical.** A 200 N force points right and up, 30° to the right of the positive vertical axis. Find $F_x,F_y$.

**P4. Cable direction.** A cable pulls point A toward point B. B is 6 m right and 8 m up from A; tension is 500 N. Find the force vector on A.

**P5. Resultant.** Concurrent forces are $\mathbf F_1=(120,-50)$ N and $\mathbf F_2=(-40,110)$ N. Find the resultant components, magnitude, and direction counterclockwise from $+x$.

## Free-body diagrams

**P6. Model the body.** A uniform horizontal 4 m beam of mass 10 kg is pinned at A at its left end and rests on a roller on a horizontal floor at B at its right end. A cable attached at the midpoint pulls right and up, 45° above horizontal, with a specified tension of 40 N. List every external force on the beam, its direction/components, and its location. Do not solve reactions yet.

## Moments and couples

**P7. Component moment.** A force $(60,-80)$ N acts at $(3,2)$ m relative to O. Find $M_O$.

**P8. Zero moment.** A 150 N force is applied at $(2,0)$ m and points along $+x$. Find its moment about O and explain the result.

**P9. Couple.** A 100 N downward force acts at $(0,0)$ m and a 100 N upward force at $(0.3,0)$ m. Find net force and net moment, including turning direction.

## Supports and loads

**P10. Sloped smooth contact.** A frictionless surface slopes 30° above $+x$. A roller contacting its upper side exerts a compressive normal reaction of magnitude $R$ on the supported body, pointing up and left. Express its components in terms of $R$.

**P11. Uniform load.** A downward load of 3 kN/m acts from $x=2$ m to $x=6$ m on a beam. Replace it with an equivalent point load and find its moment about $x=0$.

## Equilibrium

**P12. Symmetric cables.** A weightless ring carries a 200 N downward load. Two cables pull left/up and right/up, each at 45° above its respective horizontal. Find both tensions.

**P13. Point-loaded beam.** A beam has a pin A at $x=0$ and a roller on a horizontal floor at B at $x=6$ m. An 800 N downward load acts at $x=2$ m. Find $A_x,A_y,B_y$.

**P14. Cantilever.** A beam is fixed at A at $x=0$ and extends to $x=3$ m. A 400 N downward force acts at the free end. An additional clockwise couple of 200 N·m is applied to the beam. Find $A_x,A_y,M_A$.

**P15. Distributed and point loads.** A beam has a pin A at $x=0$ and a roller on a horizontal floor at B at $x=6$ m. A uniform downward load of 2 kN/m covers the entire beam. An additional 6 kN downward point load acts at $x=4$ m. Find $A_x,A_y,B_y$.

**P16. Is the assumed contact possible?** A weightless beam extends from $x=0$ to $x=4$ m. It has a pin A at $x=0$ and an unrestrained roller on a horizontal floor at B at $x=2$ m. A 100 N upward force acts at $x=4$ m. Assume $B_y$ upward, solve the equilibrium equations, and interpret whether the roller can maintain contact.

## Self-check

Before calling a problem finished, ask: Did I include every external force? Did I use the correct force location? Do force sums and moment sums vanish? Can the assumed supports physically exert the calculated reactions?
