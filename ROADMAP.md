# Study-resource roadmap

[Repository home](README.md)

This roadmap describes repository content, not a degree plan. No topic order here establishes enrollment eligibility, transfer approval, or a graduation timeline.

## Available now

- [Statics](statics/README.md): six introductory lessons, a formula sheet, 16 practice problems, and worked answers.
- [Reusable topic template](templates/topic-template.md).

## Statics extensions — not yet written

Suggested order after the current fundamentals:

1. Nonuniform distributed loads and centroids.
2. Trusses: method of joints and method of sections.
3. Frames, machines, and multiple-body FBDs.
4. Dry friction and impending motion.
5. Internal forces and shear/moment diagrams.
6. Three-dimensional forces, moments, and equilibrium.
